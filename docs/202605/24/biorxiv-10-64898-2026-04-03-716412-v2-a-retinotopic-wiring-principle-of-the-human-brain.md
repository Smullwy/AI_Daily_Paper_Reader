## 摘要
神经科学中的一个核心挑战是理解解剖连接如何将神经表征与行为联系起来。在感觉系统中，皮层区域编码外部世界的结构化地图，但仍不清楚这种表征几何是否以及如何约束活体人脑中的长程连接。这里，我们表明人类视觉皮层的结构连接保留了视网膜拓扑组织，揭示了一个视网膜拓扑连接原则：代表视觉空间中相同位置的皮层区域优先连接。在跨越2至88岁的1700多名参与者中，我们进一步表明，众所周知的知觉不对称性对应于早期视觉皮层内连接的系统性不对称性，但不存在于视觉皮层与大脑其余部分的连接中。我们引入了一种可扩展的方法，在仅靠纤维束成像不可靠时映射视网膜拓扑连接。这些发现共同证明，解剖连接保留了神经表征的几何结构，提供了一个连接大脑结构、功能与行为的一般原则。

## Abstract
Summary paragraphA central challenge in neuroscience is to understand how anatomical connectivity links neural representations to behavior. In sensory systems, cortical areas encode structured maps of the external world, yet it remains unclear whether and how this representational geometry constrains long-range connectivity in the living human brain. Here, we show that structural connectivity in the human visual cortex preserves retinotopic organization, revealing a retinotopic wiring principle; cortical regions representing the same locations in visual space preferentially connect. Across more than 1,700 participants spanning ages 2 to 88 years, we further show that well-known perceptual asymmetries correspond to systematic asymmetries in connectivity within early visual cortex, but not in connections linking visual cortex to the rest of the brain. We introduce a scalable approach to map retinotopic connectivity when tractography alone is unreliable. Together, these findings demonstrate that anatomical connectivity preserves the geometry of neural representations, providing a general principle linking brain structure, function, and behavior.
