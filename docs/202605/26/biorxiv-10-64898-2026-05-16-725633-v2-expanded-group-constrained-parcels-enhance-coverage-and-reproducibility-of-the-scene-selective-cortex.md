## 摘要
利用功能磁共振成像（fMRI）研究高级视觉皮层的一种常见方法是借助功能激活簇和解剖标志在个体被试中定义感兴趣区（ROI）。尽管这种方法富有成效，但它需要手动判断哪些簇对应于特定的经典区域，这限制了可重复性，并对信噪比较低的人群（如儿童）构成挑战。群体约束个体特异性（GSS）方法通过使用组水平脑区来约束个体特异性功能 ROI 的定义，从而降低了这种主观性。然而，原始 GSS 脑区集对枕叶地点区（OPA）的覆盖有限，且未包含新近发现的场景选择性区域。在此，我们介绍一套更新且扩展的场景选择性皮层 GSS 脑区。通过使用更大的成人样本和动态场景刺激，我们为 OPA、海马旁回地点区（PPA）和压后复合体（RSC）生成了更新的脑区，并首次为上顶叶中一个新发现的场景选择性区域（上顶叶地点区；SPA）勾画了脑区。我们在独立的成人和儿科数据集中评估了这些脑区，测试它们是否能在保持功能选择性的同时提高跨被试的覆盖度。与 Julian 及其同事的原始脑区相比，更新后的 OPA 脑区增加了跨被试覆盖度。此外，使用更新脑区定义的 ROI 在 OPA、PPA 和 RSC 中表现出同等或更高的场景选择性，表明功能 ROI 定义得到了改进且未牺牲特异性。在各个场景选择性区域，更新后的脑区能够可靠地识别场景选择性皮层，并在儿科数据中重现了典型的响应特征。这些脑区为场景加工网络提供了更全面、更可靠的覆盖，支持在成人和儿科 fMRI 数据集中进行客观且可重复的 ROI 定义。

## Abstract
A common approach for investigating high-level visual cortex with functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI) is to define regions of interest (ROIs) in individual participants using functional activation clusters and anatomical landmarks. Although highly productive, this approach requires manual decisions about which clusters correspond to specific canonical regions, limiting reproducibility and posing challenges in populations with lower signal-to-noise ratios, such as children. The Group-Constrained Subject-Specific (GSS) approach reduces this subjectivity by using group-level parcels to constrain subject-specific functional ROI definition. However, the original GSS parcel set provides limited coverage of the occipital place area (OPA) and does not include more recently characterized scene-selective regions. Here, we introduce an updated and expanded set of GSS parcels for scene-selective cortex. Using a larger adult sample and dynamic scene stimuli, we generated updated parcels for OPA, parahippocampal place area (PPA), and retrosplenial complex (RSC), and for the first time, delineated a parcel for a newly discovered scene-selective region in the superior parietal lobule (superior place area; SPA). We evaluated these parcels in independent adult and pediatric datasets by testing whether they improve cross-subject coverage while preserving functional selectivity. The updated OPA parcel increased cross-subject coverage relative to the original parcel by Julian and colleagues. Moreover, ROIs defined using the updated parcels showed equal or greater scene selectivity across OPA, PPA, and RSC, indicating improved functional ROI definition without sacrificing specificity. Across scene-selective regions, the updated parcels reliably identified scene-selective cortex and reproduced canonical response profiles and in pediatric data. These parcels provide more complete and reliable coverage of the scene-processing network, supporting objective and reproducible ROI definition across adult and pediatric fMRI datasets.
