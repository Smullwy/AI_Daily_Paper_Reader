## 摘要
本研究使用人类7T fMRI考察了视觉工作记忆的表征几何。观察者观看一张人脸与场景混合的样本图像，注意其中的人脸或场景，并判断所注意的方面是否与随后的测试图像匹配。我们发现，在视觉区和联合区，区分人脸和场景的编码维度在编码和维持阶段之间发生了旋转。在这些脑区中，互斥组合（即编码人脸而维持场景 vs. 编码场景而维持人脸）可被线性解码，表明编码和维持阶段使用不同的子空间来表征视觉信息。原则上，这种高维、灵活的几何结构可以保护维持的视觉信息免受新的输入干扰。同时，跨阶段稳健的交叉解码被观察到，反映了所表征内容的稳定性和泛化性。表征灵活性与稳定性之间的平衡沿皮层层级变化：早期感觉区更强调灵活性，而跨模态区域表现出更大的稳定性。

## Abstract
This study examined the representational geometry of visual working memory using human 7T fMRI. Observers viewed a facescene blended sample, attended to either the face or scene, and judged whether the attended aspect matched a subsequent test image. We found that the coding dimension distinguishing faces and scenes rotated between encoding and maintenance phases, in both visual and association areas. In these regions, exclusive combinations (i.e., encoded face and maintained scene vs. encoded scene and maintained face) were linearly decodable, indicating encoding and maintenance phases represent visual information using distinct subspaces. Such high-dimensional, flexible geometry can, in principle, protect maintained visual information from incoming input. At the same time, robust crossdecoding was observed across phases, reflecting the stability and generalizability of the represented contents. The balance between representational flexibility and stability varied along the cortical hierarchy: early sensory regions emphasized flexibility, whereas transmodal regions showed greater stability.
