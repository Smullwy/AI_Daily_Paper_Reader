## 摘要
感知源于感觉刺激的前馈加工与内在神经活动之间的动态相互作用，后者常被视为噪声。为了不断根据机体当前需求实时调整感知过程，内在动力学——而非仅仅是噪声——被认为反映了先验预期、任务需求以及注意焦点。在此，我们识别了注意状态的一种新特征：上丘（SC）——一个整合自下而上视觉输入与自上而下信号的中脑枢纽——内的内在集体神经活动通过与感觉输入动态相互作用来调节视觉空间注意。我们表明，这些内在动力学将细胞活动组织成结构化的、团块状的特征，这些特征在时间和地形上具有局域性，强度上可与感觉诱发反应相媲美。这些特征随着动物学会参与视觉检测任务而出现，并能逐试次地预测行为结果。尽管独立于感觉输入或外显行为，这些特征在注意状态下可被招募以将视觉反应增强四倍，生成一个与反应时间和行为结果相一致的“动态显著性地图”。计算模型表明，可调的团块状特征可由上丘内的局部兴奋-抑制相互作用产生，并增强感觉诱发反应，与观察结果一致。总之，我们的结果识别了一种依赖状态的内在群体活动，它与感觉输入相互作用，产生了一种类似于显著性地图的表征，该表征与灵活的目标导向行为相关。

## Abstract
Perception results from a dynamic interplay between the feedforward processing of sensory stimuli and intrinsic neural activity 1, which is often dismissed as noise 2. To tailor perceptual processes to the organisms current needs on a continuous, moment-to-moment basis, intrinsic dynamics - rather than just being noise - have been suggested to reflect prior expectations 3,4, task demands 5, and attentional focus 6. Here, we identify a novel signature of attentive state in which intrinsic, collective neural activity modulates visuospatial attention by dynamically interacting with sensory input within the superior colliculus (SC), a midbrain hub that integrates bottom-up visual input with top-down signals 7. We show that these intrinsic dynamics organize cell activity into structured, blob-like features that are temporally and topographically localized and that rival sensory-evoked responses in strength. These features emerge as animals learn to engage in the visual detection task, and predict behavioral outcomes on a trial-by-trial basis. Although independent of sensory input or overt behavior, the features can be recruited to enhance visual responses four-fold in the attentive state, generating a "dynamic saliency map" that aligns with reaction times and behavioral outcomes. A computational model indicates that tunable blob-like features can arise from local excitatory-inhibitory interactions within the SC, and enhance sensory-evoked responses in line with observations. Together, our results identify state-dependent intrinsic population activity that interacts with sensory input to give rise to a saliency-map-like representation linked to flexible, goal-directed behavior.
