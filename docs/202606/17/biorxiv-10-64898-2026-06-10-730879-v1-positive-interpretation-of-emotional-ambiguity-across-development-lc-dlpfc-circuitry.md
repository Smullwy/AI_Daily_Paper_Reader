## 摘要
情绪模糊的面部表情为研究不确定情感信息如何被解析为分类判断提供了一个易处理的模型。这里我们检验了该过程中的发展差异是否反映在蓝斑（LC）回路中，以及这些神经效应如何与解释偏向的行为和决策过程指标相关。从儿童晚期到中年期的参与者完成了在fMRI扫描过程中的从愤怒到快乐的面部渐变任务。行为反应使用信号检测论和漂移扩散模型进行分析，fMRI数据使用以LC为种子点的广义心理生理交互分析和试次级神经模型进行分析。积极偏向随发展系统性增加。漂移扩散分析显示，这一变化具体由漂移截距（证据累积的一个刺激无关评价成分）捕捉，而非由知觉敏感性、起点或反应谨慎性捕捉。在神经层面，LC-dlPFC耦合与积极偏向之间的关联在中年人中最为强健。试次级分析进一步表明，dlPFC携带了更清晰的逐时评价信号，而LC通过更慢的时间特性（以赫斯特指数为指标）塑造了与偏向相关的皮层耦合。重要的是，更高的LC结构完整性（对比噪声比）与更低的LC时间持续性以及进而更负的LC-dlPFC耦合相关。总之，这些发现表明，积极解释的年龄相关差异并非仅仅源于知觉敏感性或反应倾向的差异，而是反映了由LC-dlPFC回路支持的评估性证据累积的年龄渐变变异。这项工作扩展了近期表明LC-前额叶连接参与模糊处理的证据，并将评估性漂移偏向确定为情绪模糊性解析的一个年龄渐变的回路层面特征。

意义陈述：大脑在发展过程中如何解析情绪模糊面孔尚不清楚。通过使用心理测量学、漂移扩散和fMRI分析对青少年、新兴成人和中年人进行研究，我们表明，与年龄相关的积极解释增加可由漂移截距（证据累积的一个刺激无关评价成分）解释，而非由知觉敏感性或起点偏差解释。LC-dlPFC耦合跟踪了同一偏差维度。试次级分析表明，dlPFC携带了更清晰的逐时评价信号，而LC通过更慢的时间特性贡献于偏差相关耦合。这些发现将评估性漂移偏差确定为一个可测量的回路关联的情绪模糊解析机制。

## Abstract
Emotionally ambiguous facial expressions provide a tractable model for studying how uncertain affective information is resolved into categorical judgments. Here we tested whether developmental differences in this process are reflected in locus coeruleus (LC) circuitry and how these neural effects relate to behavioral and decision-process indices of interpretive bias. Participants from late childhood through middle-aged adulthood completed an angry-to-happy facial morph task during fMRI. Behavioral responses were analyzed using signal detection theory, and drift-diffusion modeling, and fMRI data were analyzed using LC-seeded generalized psychophysiological interaction and trial-level neural models. Positive bias increased systematically across development. Drift-diffusion analyses showed that this shift was captured specifically by drift intercept, a stimulus-independent evaluative component of evidence accumulation, rather than by perceptual sensitivity, starting point, or response caution. At the neural level, the association between LC-dlPFC coupling and positive bias was strongest in middle-aged adults. Trial-level analyses further indicated that dlPFC carried the clearer moment-to-moment evaluative signal, whereas LC contributed through slower temporal properties, indexed by the Hurst exponent, that shaped bias-related cortical coupling. Importantly, higher LC structural integrity (contrast-to-noise ratio) was associated with lower LC temporal persistence and, in turn, more negative LC-dlPFC coupling. Together, these findings suggest that age-related differences in positive interpretation are not simply differences in perceptual sensitivity or response tendency, but reflect age-graded variation in evaluative evidence accumulation supported by LC-dlPFC circuitry. This work extends recent evidence implicating LC-prefrontal connectivity in ambiguity processing and identifies evaluative drift bias as an age-graded circuit-level signature of emotional ambiguity resolution.

Significance StatementHow the brain resolves emotionally ambiguous faces across development is poorly understood. Using psychometric, drift-diffusion, and fMRI analyses in youth, emerging adults, and middle-aged adults, we show that age-related increases in positive interpretation are explained by drift intercept, a stimulus-independent evaluative component of evidence accumulation, rather than by perceptual sensitivity or starting-point bias. LC-dlPFC coupling tracked this same bias dimension. Trial-level analyses suggested that dlPFC carried the clearer moment-to-moment evaluative signal, whereas LC contributed through slower temporal properties of bias-related coupling. These findings identify evaluative drift bias as a measurable circuit-linked mechanism of emotional ambiguity resolution.
