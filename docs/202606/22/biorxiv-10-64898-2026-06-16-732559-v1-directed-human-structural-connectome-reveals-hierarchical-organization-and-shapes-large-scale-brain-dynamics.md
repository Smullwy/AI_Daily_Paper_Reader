## 摘要
人类结构连接组通常源自弥散加权成像（DWI）和纤维束成像，提供了全脑布线的大尺度描述，并作为网络神经科学、大规模脑模拟和个性化数字大脑双胞胎的结构基础。然而，由纤维束成像得到的连接组从根本上受限于无法区分传入与传出连接，生成的网络是无向的，因此无法洞察由解剖连接的方向性所决定的层级组织。本研究通过利用跨物种连接蓝图，将示踪剂衍生的投射模式从猕猴转移到人类，引入了一个有向人类结构连接组（DHSC）。DHSC的拓扑分析显示出生物学合理性、小世界网络组织和基于方向性的层级结构，从而揭示了人脑网络的层级组织。在脑动力学方面，方向性的引入重塑了感觉输入的传播和持久性。DHSC还最好地捕捉了刺激诱发脑活动的经验时空动力学。这些发现表明，解剖方向性是大尺度脑组织和动力学的关键决定因素。这提供证据表明，有向连接组在人脑大规模模拟中可能具有潜在优势。所得DHSC以及所有相关分析和数据均已公开可用。

## Abstract
The human structural connectome, most commonly derived from diffusion-weighted imaging (DWI) and tractography, provides a macroscopic description of whole-brain wiring and serves as the structural foundation of network neuroscience, large-scale brain simulations, and personalized digital brain twins. However, tractography-derived connectomes are fundamentally limited by their inability to distinguish afferent from efferent connections, yielding networks that are undirected and therefore blind to the hierarchical organization imposed by the directionality of anatomical connections. In this study, we introduce a directed human structural connectome (DHSC) by transferring tracer-derived projection patterns from macaque to human using cross-species connectivity blueprints. Topological analysis of the DHSC manifests biological plausibility, a small-world network organization, and a directionality-based hierarchy, which offer the hierarchical organization of human brain networks. In the context of brain dynamics, the introduction of directionality reshapes the propagation and persistence of sensory inputs. DHSC also best captures the empirical spatiotemporal dynamics of stimulus-evoked brain activity. The findings demonstrate that anatomical directionality is a critical determinant of large-scale brain organization and dynamics. This provides evidence that directed connectome may offer potential advantages in large-scale simulations of the human brain. The resulting DHSC, along with all related analyses and data are openly available.
