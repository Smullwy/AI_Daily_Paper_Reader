## 摘要
有意识感知的神经关联仍然存在争议，特别是关于前额叶皮层等感觉外区域的作用。一种有前景的方法是研究任务相关和任务无关情境下神经处理的动力学特性。先前的一项脑电图研究表明，有意识的感知与全或无的晚期激活相关，即使在没有任务的情况下也会产生分岔动力学。在这里，我们使用功能磁共振成像和近阈值听觉刺激来探究哪些大脑网络产生了这些分岔，以及它们如何根据任务而不同。在两种情境下，刺激强度会调节横跨感觉和感觉外区域（包括前额叶皮层）的广泛网络内的活动。这些网络表现出共享和独特的组成部分。单试次建模进一步揭示了初级感觉皮层之外的分岔动力学，并使得在两种情境下都能对单试次的有意识感知进行预测。这些发现解决了先前矛盾的结果，并揭示了与任务无关的有意识感知背后的共同网络和动力学。

## Abstract
The neural correlates of conscious perception remain debated, particularly regarding the role of extra-sensory regions such as the prefrontal cortex. One promising approach is to study the dynamical properties of neural processing in task-related and task-free contexts. A previous EEG study showed that conscious perception is associated with all-or-none late activations, giving rise to bifurcation dynamics even without a task. Here we used fMRI and near-threshold auditory stimulation to ask which brain networks give rise to these bifurcations, and how they differ depending on task. In both contexts, stimulus intensity modulated activity within broad networks spanning sensory and extra-sensory regions, including the prefrontal cortex. These networks showed both shared and distinct components. Single-trial modelling further revealed bifurcation dynamics beyond primary sensory cortices and enabled single-trial prediction of conscious perception in both contexts. These findings resolve previous conflicting results and reveal common networks and dynamics underlying conscious perception irrespective of task.
