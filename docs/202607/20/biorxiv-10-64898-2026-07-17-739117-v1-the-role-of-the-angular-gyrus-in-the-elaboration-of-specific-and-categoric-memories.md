## 摘要
本研究探讨了角回及其亚区在特定和类别自传体记忆精细加工中的作用。采用线索词功能磁共振成像范式，三十九名参与者提取并精细加工了情景性（特定）和语义性（类别）记忆，并对每次回忆的细节量进行评分。参数分析显示，无论记忆类型如何，角回活动，尤其是后部角回，与提取的细节量呈正相关。与类别记忆相比，特定记忆在右侧角回亚区诱发了更强的激活。这些发现支持角回参与情景性和语义性记忆提取，并提示其亚区之间存在功能分化。

## Abstract
This study investigated the role of the angular gyrus (AG) and its subregions in the elaboration of specific and categoric autobiographical memories (AMs). Using a cue-word fMRI paradigm, thirty-nice participants retrieved and elaborated on episodic (specific) and semantic (categoric) memories, rating the amount of detail of each recollection. Parametric analyses revealed that AG activity, particularly in posterior AG, was positively associated with the amount of detail retrieved, regardless of memory type. Specific memories elicited greater activation in right AG subregions compared to categoric memories. These findings support the AG's involvement in both episodic and semantic memory retrieval and suggest functional differentiation between its subregions.
